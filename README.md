**English** · [简体中文](README.zh.md) · [繁體中文](README.zh-TW.md) · [日本語](README.ja.md)

RealSub is a Windows app that shows live captions (real-time subtitles) for whatever your PC is playing — videos, streams, games and more. Speech is recognized on your own machine, and RealSub can translate between Japanese, English and Chinese, showing the result in a floating subtitle bar.

It can listen to the whole system's audio or to a single app you choose, and you can scroll back through earlier subtitles. The Full Version adds SRT/MP3 export. The base app is free.
